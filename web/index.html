Closing the licence — the discharge certificate

An Export Obligation Discharge Certificate is what releases the bond and the bank guarantee you gave Customs. Until it is in hand the licence is open, however much has been exported. This tool assembles the file and says whether it hangs together. It files nothing.

Figures applied, and where they come from
